Born deaf.
A new possibility.

Article, video & evidence

Abdu and Sumaya have a rare inherited form of deafness. A gene therapy was designed to give cells in the inner ear the instructions needed to pass sound signals to the hearing nerve.

What is Otarmeni?It is the brand name of a one-time gene therapy for specifically eligible people with OTOF-related hearing loss. It is delivered into the inner ear during surgery and does not treat every form of deafness.
